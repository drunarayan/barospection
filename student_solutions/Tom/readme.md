### Tom Smith
#### [on team with Ayush](../Ayush)
Hello all, I am Thomas C. Smith, Director of the Dark Ridge Observatory located in the Sacramento Mounts of southeast New Mexico. I am at an elevation of 7100 feet. No active observing/science is being conducted at this time. I am a coauthor of the AAVSO Photometric All-Sky Survey (APASS). I am currently involved in the TESS SG3 group doing high-precision speckle interferometry studies of potential exoplanet targets as well as an active member of the Boyce-Astro BRIEF programs. I am a board member for the Institute for Student Astronomical Research (InStAR).
